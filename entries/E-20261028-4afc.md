Conformal Cascade: Distribution-Free Accuracy Guarantees for Multi-Tier LLM Inference adopts a lower-tier answer when its calibrated prediction set is a singleton. Under the conformal calibration assumptions, the bound concerns joint acceptance and error, rather than error conditional on acceptance.
